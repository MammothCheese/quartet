QUARTET for the M-VAVE FM-1

Your first song,
from nothing.

A beat, a bassline, chords and a melody in A minor. Every key is drawn for you, and it only uses the white keys.

That's what you'll make. One step per screen: do it, tick it, next. Swipe to go back.

Step 0 · 2 min

Get it running, start empty.

  • Install it: download, then the Firmware Hub (how). Or play it in your browser.
  • On the home screen, hold REC until it says CLEAR ALL?
  • Press REC again. The demo is gone; your saved projects aren't touched.
BROWSER KEYS

U HOME   hold ] REC, then ]   1 2 octave

Step 1 · 2 min

Kick, snare, hats.

  • KNOB 1 to 90 BPM, KNOB 2 to SW 53%.
  • SELECT to DRUMS, then SEQ.
  • A drum's black key, then its steps on the white keys:
BROWSER KEYS

drag the knobs   ←→ SELECT   P SEQ   [ PLAY   kick A snare S hat D   steps: the letters under the chart

Step 2 · 2 min

Four chords, eight notes.

  • HOME, SELECT to BASS, ALGORITHM to MOOG BASS.
  • SEQ. KNOB 1 to BARS 4, KNOB 2 to HALF.
  • Press these, in order. The cursor moves on by itself.

A A · F F · C C · G G

Wrong note? ALGORITHM back to it, play the right key.

BROWSER KEYS

U HOME   ↑↓ ALGORITHM   E E Q Q X X W W

Step 3 · 2 min

Three keys at once.

  • HOME, SELECT to PAD. Keep STRINGS.
  • SEQ. BARS 4, KNOB 2 to WHOLE.
  • Hold each chord's three keys together, let go:

1 · Am

2 · F

3 · C

4 · G

BROWSER KEYS

EXV · EXB · WXV · WZC (all three at once)

Step 4 · 3 min

Up each chord, and back.

  • HOME, SELECT to LEAD. Keep PIANO.
  • SEQ. BARS 4, KNOB 2 to QUARTER.
  • Sixteen notes, one at a time, in number order:

1 · Am

2 · F

3 · C

4 · G

BROWSER KEYS

Step 5 · 1 min

Space around the piano.

FX SEL ENV LFO are the chosen player's four pedals: Reverb, Delay, Distortion, Chorus.

  • HOME and PLAY. With the lead chosen, tap FX and SEL.
  • SELECT to PAD and tap FX: reverb on the pad too.

More: hold a pedal button to open it. KNOB 2–4 are its knobs, and PRESETS and ALGORITHM swap it for one of 37.

BROWSER KEYS

7 FX   8 SEL   9 ENV   0 LFO

Step 6 · 3 min

A fill every fourth bar.

  • HOME, SELECT to DRUMS. Hold ARP, turn PRESETS one click: the beat is copied to B.
  • SEQ, the snare's black key, white keys 14 15 16.
  • SEQ again: SUPER SEQ. KNOB 1 ON, KNOB 4 LEN 4.
  • ALGORITHM to BOX 4, KNOB 3 to B. PLAY.

Perform it: GLO, then FX SEL ENV LFO mute and unmute the players.

BROWSER KEYS

hold O (ARP) + PgDn   snare S, steps R T Y   P SEQ   ↑↓ box   = GLO

Step 7 · 30 s

REC twice keeps it.

Your work is remembered on its own a couple of seconds after you stop. To keep this song for good:

  • Press SAVE.
  • KNOB 1 to a slot, 1–4.
  • REC, then REC again within three seconds.
BROWSER KEYS

I SAVE   ] ] save   [ [ load. Saves stay in this browser.

That's it

You made a song.

  • HOME always goes back home.
  • SELECT is the player, PRESETS the pattern.
  • ALGORITHM moves within a page, never off it.
  • Tap a pedal or ARP: on/off. Hold: open it.
  • REC twice confirms anything that keeps or replaces work.
