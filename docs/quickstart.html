Quick start · QUARTET for the M-VAVE FM-1

Your first song,
from nothing.

Start with an empty FM-1 and finish with a four-part song: a beat, a bassline, chords and a melody, all in A minor. Every key to press is drawn for you. It only uses the white keys, so it can't go wrong. About fifteen minutes.

Step 1 · A beat · 2 min

Kick, snare, hats.
Tap them in.

  • On the home screen, turn KNOB 1 down to 90 BPM. For a touch of swing: GLO twice (the SONG page), KNOB 3 to 53%, then HOME.
  • Turn SELECT to DRUMS, the bottom line. They're already the 808 kit.
  • Press SEQ. Now the black keys choose a drum and the 16 white keys are the 16 steps.
  • Press a drum's black key, then tap its steps on the white keys:

Step 2 · A bassline · 2 min

Four bars,
eight notes.

The song goes round four chords: A minor, F, C, G. The bass plays each one's note, twice.

  • Press HOME, turn SELECT to BASS, then ALGORITHM until it says MOOG BASS.
  • Press SEQ. Turn KNOB 1 to BARS 4 and KNOB 2 until the bottom line says HALF.
  • Press these keys in order, one at a time. After each note the cursor jumps on by itself.

A A · F F · C C · G G

Climb up each chord,

The lead plays an octave above the keys, so this piano sparkles over the pad: up through each chord's three notes, then back one.

  • Press HOME and turn SELECT to LEAD. Keep PIANO.
  • Press SEQ. KNOB 1 to BARS 4, KNOB 2 to QUARTER.

Step 5 · Pedals · 1 min

Space around the piano,
a wash on the pad.

The top buttons FX SEL ENV LFO are the chosen player's four pedals: Reverb, Delay, Distortion and Chorus to begin with.

  • Press HOME and PLAY. With the lead chosen, tap FX (reverb) and SEL (delay).The delay's echoes fall between the notes.
  • Turn SELECT to PAD and tap FX: reverb on the pad too.

Go further: hold a pedal button for half a second to open it. KNOB 2–4 turn its knobs, and PRESETS and ALGORITHM swap it for one of 40. Try Old Media on the pad for a lo-fi sound.

Step 6 · Make it a song · 3 min

A drum fill
every fourth bar.

Make a second drum pattern with a fill, then let SUPER SEQ play it at the end of every round of chords.

  • Press HOME, SELECT to DRUMS. Hold ARP and turn PRESETS one click: the beat is copied into pattern B.
  • Press SEQ, the snare's black key, then white keys 14, 15 and 16: a snare roll.
  • Press SEQ again for SUPER SEQ. KNOB 1 to ON, KNOB 4 to LEN 4.
  • ALGORITHM to BOX 4 on the drum row, and KNOB 3 to B. Then PLAY.

DRUMS row: A  A  A  B  (each box a bar). The others play their 4-bar A all the way through.

Perform it: GLO opens the mixer, where FX SEL ENV LFO mute lead, pad, bass and drums. Start with only the drums and bring the others in one by one.

Step 7 · Save it · 30 s

Four slots.
REC twice keeps it.

QUARTET remembers your work on its own a couple of seconds after you stop. To keep a song for good, save it to one of four project slots.

  • Press SAVE.
  • Turn KNOB 1 to choose slot 1–4.
  • Press REC, then REC again within three seconds.PLAY twice loads a slot instead. SAVE closes the page.

That's it

You made a song.
Five rules for the rest.

  • HOME always takes you back to the home screen.
  • SELECT is always the player. PRESETS is the pattern, or the page's own thing (a pedal, a song).
  • ALGORITHM moves around within a page. It never takes you to another one.
  • Tap a pedal button or ARP to switch it on or off. Hold it to open it.
  • REC twice confirms anything that keeps or replaces work.

Next: try other sounds with ALGORITHM, shape them with EDIT, write a chorus in pattern B, and hold HOME and turn PRESETS to choose a look. Everything is in the full guide.
