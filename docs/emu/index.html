Play it in your browser

The real QUARTET firmware, running right here. Click the buttons and keys, or use your computer's keyboard. Your songs are kept in this browser.

Sound starts when you press it.

The keys

Z X C V B N M , . / are the ten white keys in the middle (B3 to D5).

The row above, A to ', plays the black keys between them, just as on a piano. 1 2 shift the octave.

The buttons

7 8 9 0 - = FX SEL ENV LFO EDIT GLO (the top row)

U I O P [ ] HOME SAVE ARP SEQ PLAY REC   1 2 OCT− OCT+

←→ SELECT   ↑↓ ALGORITHM   PgUpPgDn PRESETS. Hold a key for combos (HOME + PRESETS), or shift-click a button.

The knobs

Drag a knob up or down, or scroll on it. MASTER is the volume.

New here? The quick start takes five minutes: press PLAY first.
