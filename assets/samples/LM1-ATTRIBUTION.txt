QUARTET's LM-1 kit plays samples from:

  "Linn LM1 (LM-1) Drum Set" by rossf
  https://freesound.org/people/rossf/packs/4919/
  License: Sampling+ 1.0, http://creativecommons.org/licenses/sampling+/1.0/

The six sounds used, as QUARTET's LM-1 rows:
  KICK    LM1 Kick.wav       https://freesound.org/s/75842/
  SNARE   LM1 Snare.wav      https://freesound.org/s/75843/
  HAT     LM1 Hat Cl.wav     https://freesound.org/s/75840/
  OPEN    LM1 Hat Open.wav   https://freesound.org/s/75841/
  TOM L   LM1 Tom Lo.wav     https://freesound.org/s/75846/   (the CLAP row)
  TOM H   LM1 Tom Hi.wav     https://freesound.org/s/75845/   (the PERC row)

What was done to them (tools/samples2c.py): the silent tail of each trimmed (below -60 dB of its peak, a 5 ms fade),
then stored as 8-bit mu-law at 44.1 kHz in firmware/gen/lm1_samples.h. QUARTET plays them at the speed and volume
the kit's settings give.

QUARTET is free software and is shared without charge. The sounds are not part of QUARTET's GPL: they stay under
Sampling+ 1.0, which lets you share whole copies only for noncommercial purposes, credit the author, and not use them
to advertise anything but what you make with them. If you use these sounds outside QUARTET, take them from the
source above and follow its terms. rossf recorded them from a Linn LM-1; Linn Electronics' rights in the original
machine's sounds are not covered by this licence.
